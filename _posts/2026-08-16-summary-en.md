## [SSOG-Attention: Sub-Quadratic Attention via Separable Gaussians](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vpt6ay/ssogattention_sum_of_separable_gaussians_as_a/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

SSOG-Attention introduces a novel attention mechanism that approximates scaled dot-product attention using a sum of separable Gaussians, reducing complexity from O(N²·d) to O(N·√N·d). Experiments show it outperforms SDPA on CIFAR-100 and matches performance on ImageNet with faster convergence. This work addresses the quadratic complexity bottleneck of standard attention, enabling more efficient transformers for long sequences and large-scale vision tasks. It offers a promising alternative to existing sub-quadratic methods, potentially impacting the design of future efficient architectures. The method learns a few Gaussian atoms per head and steers them based on the query token, leveraging the separability of Gaussians to achieve sub-quadratic complexity. The authors provide a blog post and open-source repository with additional results and ablations.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/4rtemi5 · Aug 16, 10:06

**Background**: Scaled dot-product attention (SDPA) computes attention scores between all query-key pairs, leading to O(N²) complexity, which becomes prohibitive for long sequences. Sub-quadratic attention methods aim to reduce this complexity while maintaining performance. Separable Gaussians are functions that can be factored into lower-dimensional components, enabling efficient computation.

BDH-CQ, a 150M-parameter reasoning system, combines in-context learning with recurrent latent reasoning, achieving 29.5% pass@2 on ARC-AGI-1 at a cost of $0.00070 per task, breaking the previous cost-accuracy Pareto frontier. 这表明高效的非语言推理可以在ARC-AGI-1等具有挑战性的基准上取得有竞争力的性能，可能推动更经济高效的AI推理系统，并挑战大型语言模型在推理任务中的主导地位。 The model updates its recurrent memory with demonstrations at inference time and solves queries through iterative computation in a high-dimensional latent space, without decoding intermediate reasoning states into language. Neither task identifiers nor demonstration pairs are used in training, and no parameters are updated at inference time.

The Pareto frontier in AI represents the trade-off between cost and accuracy, where BDH-CQ claims to achieve a new optimal point. Recurrent latent reasoning involves maintaining a hidden state that evolves over time, allowing the model to reason without explicit verbalization.
