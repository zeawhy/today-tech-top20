Its 'innovation tokens' concept is widely cited and remains relevant in discussions about technology strategy and managing technical debt. The essay introduces the idea that every company has roughly three 'innovation tokens' to spend over a long period, and that choosing boring technology for most things is free. It emphasizes that novelty should be reserved for areas where it provides a real competitive edge, rather than being adopted for its own sake.

## [WorldProof: Diagnosing World-Model Failures and Pixel Metric Limits](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vnliv7/worldproof_diagnosing_where_worldmodel/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The author released WorldProof, an open-source tool for diagnosing world models, and demonstrated that pixel metrics like SSIM and PSNR often cannot rank models on real robot video, as a static baseline achieves high scores (0.983 SSIM, 53.9 dB PSNR) with flat error over horizons. This finding challenges the common practice of using pixel metrics to evaluate world models, potentially affecting how researchers assess model quality and compare approaches.
