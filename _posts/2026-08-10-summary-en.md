## [First Viable Bacteriophage Genomes Generated by AI Language Models](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers used the genome language models Evo 1 and Evo 2 to generate complete bacteriophage genomes, achieving the first generative design of viable phages with experimental validation. Experimental testing yielded 16 viable phages with substantial evolutionary novelty, using the lytic phage ΦX174 as the design template. This breakthrough demonstrates that AI can design functional whole genomes, not just individual proteins, opening new avenues for synthetic biology and phage therapy. It could accelerate the development of customized bacteriophages to combat antibiotic-resistant bacteria, with broad implications for medicine and biotechnology. The generated phages exhibited target host tropism and a cocktail of them rapidly overcame ΦX174-resistant Escherichia coli strains. The work was published in Science (DOI: 10.1126/science.aec2657) and builds on the Evo 2 model, which is a generalist genome language model trained across all domains of life.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

**Background**: Genome language models are AI systems trained on vast genetic sequence libraries, similar to text-based models like ChatGPT but for DNA. They can predict variant effects and generate new sequences, but until now, generating functional whole genomes had not been demonstrated. Bacteriophages are viruses that infect bacteria, and their design could enable targeted therapies against bacterial infections.

## [Mechanistic Explanation of Prompt Injection and Role-Based Defense](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjvzm4/a_mechanistic_explanation_of_prompt_injection_and/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The post provides a mechanistic explanation of prompt injection, framing it as a failure of role separation in LLMs, and argues that studying roles is key to mitigating this vulnerability. Prompt injection is a critical security issue for LLM-based applications, and understanding its underlying mechanisms can lead to more robust defenses. This perspective shifts focus from ad-hoc filtering to principled role-based design, which could influence how future models are built and secured. The post likely discusses how LLMs cannot inherently distinguish between system instructions and user input because both are natural language strings, leading to injection attacks. It suggests that explicitly modeling roles (e.g., system vs. user) could help mitigate this, possibly through architectural changes or training techniques.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/katxwoods · Aug 9, 17:36

**Background**: Prompt injection is a type of code injection attack that exploits the fact that LLMs treat all input as instructions, allowing malicious prompts to override system instructions. Mechanistic interpretability aims to reverse-engineer neural networks to understand their internal circuits, which could reveal how roles are represented and manipulated. The OWASP Gen AI Security Project lists prompt injection as the top LLM risk, highlighting its severity.

## [World's Largest Single AI Computing Facility Goes Live in Inner Mongolia](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367666.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 6, Envision Group announced the official operation of the Vision Ulanqab Galaxy Base in Inner Mongolia, the world's largest single AI computing facility. The base supports million-GPU parallel computing with a planned total capacity of 2GW and over 80% green energy usage. This milestone underscores China's push to build massive AI infrastructure, leveraging western regions' energy and cost advantages. It could set a benchmark for scalable, green-powered AI data centers, impacting the global AI computing landscape. The base covers 120,000 square meters and is located in Ulanqab, a national 'East Data, West Computing' hub, about 240 km from Beijing with a data transmission latency of only 4.2 ms. Electricity prices are about 50% lower than in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region, and it is the first flagship project of Envision's 'Gobi Mission' plan.

**Background**: The 'East Data, West Computing' project is a Chinese national initiative to channel eastern computing demands to western regions, leveraging their abundant renewable energy and cooler climates. GPUs are specialized processors designed for massive parallel computing, essential for training large AI models. Green electricity refers to power generated from renewable sources like wind and solar, which is increasingly important for reducing the carbon footprint of data centers.
