## [Inouye Telescope Observes Kelvin-Helmholtz Instability on Sun](https://nso.edu/press-release/nsf-inouye-solar-telescope-enables-major-discovery-of-a-hidden-solar-process/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

An international team using the NSF Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope has directly observed Kelvin-Helmholtz instability (KHI) on the Sun's surface, confirming a long-held theory about energy dissipation in solar plasma. The findings were published in a Nature paper. This observation is a major breakthrough in solar physics, as KHI is believed to be critical for understanding how energy dissipates in the Sun, influencing the formation of sunspots and flares. It validates decades of theoretical and simulation work and opens new avenues for studying solar dynamics and space weather. The observations were made with the 4-meter Inouye Solar Telescope on Maui, combined with computer simulations. The discovery involved detecting tiny swirling patterns, like small whirlpools, on the Sun's surface, which are signatures of KHI.

hackernews · neversaydie · Aug 5, 15:33 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184355)

**Background**: Kelvin-Helmholtz instability occurs when there is velocity shear in a fluid or across the interface between two fluids, leading to characteristic swirling patterns. It is commonly seen in cloud formations on Earth and in planetary atmospheres, but observing it on the Sun's surface has been challenging due to the need for high-resolution telescopes. The Inouye Solar Telescope, the world's largest solar telescope, provides the necessary resolution to detect such small-scale features.

**Background**: Pareto optimality, named after economist Vilfredo Pareto, describes a state where no individual can be made better off without making someone else worse off. ## [Bidirectional Diffusion Models Predict Their Own Rollout Errors](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vh2gn1/roundtrip_consistency_bidirectional_diffusion/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new paper introduces a bidirectional conditional latent diffusion model that can predict its own rollout errors via round-trip consistency, providing a measurement-free test-time error signal. The model outperforms specialist models trained in only one direction. This work addresses a critical challenge in autoregressive generative models: estimating rollout error without ground truth. It offers a self-supervised method that could improve reliability in applications like video generation and digital twins of physical systems. The method trains a single latent diffusion model to step a dynamical system forward or backward via a direction flag. The round-trip discrepancy—rolling forward then backward—serves as a self-supervised proxy for the unobservable rollout error, requiring no ensembles, held-out data, or governing equations.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Clean-Hovercraft5825 · Aug 6, 12:10

**Background**: Latent diffusion models (LDMs) are generative models that perform diffusion in a compressed latent space, widely used in image and video generation. Autoregressive models, including diffusion-based ones, accumulate errors over long rollouts, but at deployment there is no ground truth to measure against. Round-trip consistency is a paradigm that ensures bidirectional consistency via paired forward and reverse mappings, which this work applies to error estimation.

## [US Probes Chinese AI Firms' Offshore Access to Nvidia Chips](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/us-reviews-china-s-offshore-access-to-nvidia-chips-after-ai-breakthroughs) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The US Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) has launched a systematic review of how Chinese AI companies access Nvidia chips overseas, including through remote cloud computing. This follows the release of Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 model, which a White House official accused of illegally obtaining Nvidia chips via Thailand. This review could reshape the enforcement of US export controls on advanced AI chips, potentially extending restrictions to cloud-based access. It affects major AI companies and cloud providers, and may escalate US-China tech tensions, impacting global AI supply chains. BIS is compiling two lists: one of black-market locations suspected of smuggling restricted chips into China, and another of countries where Chinese firms remotely rent chips. The legal authority to restrict such cloud computing agreements is unclear, and a bipartisan House bill to grant that power faces opposition from Nvidia and other tech companies.
