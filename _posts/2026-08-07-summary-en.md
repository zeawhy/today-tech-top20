## [Chinese-led team confirms existence of glueballs after 15-year search](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 6, 2026, the BESIII collaboration announced the first experimental confirmation of glueballs, a new form of matter. The team identified the particle X(2370) as a glueball based on its quantum properties and new decay modes. This is a major milestone in particle physics, as glueballs are a key prediction of the Standard Model and have remained unobserved for nearly 50 years. Confirming their existence validates the theory of quantum chromodynamics and opens new avenues for studying the strong force. The discovery was made using the BESIII detector at the Beijing Electron-Positron Collider. The X(2370) particle was first observed in 2011, and its spin-parity and flavor-singlet properties were measured in 2024 and 2026, confirming its glueball nature.

**Background**: In particle physics, glueballs are hypothetical composite particles consisting solely of gluons, the force carriers of the strong interaction. Unlike ordinary hadrons, glueballs contain no valence quarks. The Standard Model predicts their existence, but they have been notoriously difficult to observe experimentally.

The Inouye Solar Telescope, with its 4-meter mirror, is the world's largest solar telescope and provides unprecedented views of the Sun.

## [Bidirectional Diffusion Models Predict Their Own Rollout Errors](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vh2gn1/roundtrip_consistency_bidirectional_diffusion/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new paper introduces a bidirectional conditional latent diffusion model that uses round-trip consistency to predict its own rollout errors in dynamical systems without ground truth, achieving better accuracy than specialist models. This method provides a measurement-free, self-supervised error signal for autoregressive generative models, which is crucial for long-horizon predictions in applications like video generation and digital twins. It could reduce reliance on ensembles or held-out data and improve model reliability. The model steps a dynamical system forward or backward via a direction flag, and the round-trip discrepancy (forward then backward) serves as a self-supervised proxy for rollout error.
