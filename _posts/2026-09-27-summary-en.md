## [DeepSeek Unveils DSec Sandbox Infrastructure for Agent Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22978) ⭐️ 8.0/10

DeepSeek published an arXiv paper introducing DeepSeek Elastic Compute (DSec), a production sandbox platform that exposes FnCall, container, microVM, and full-VM backends through a unified SDK. The system reportedly achieves 380,000 concurrent sandboxes across 160 Epyc-based server nodes, and is co-designed with DeepSeek's reinforcement learning framework starting with DeepSeek-V4.1. As AI agents grow more capable, safe and scalable sandboxing is becoming critical infrastructure for both training and deployment, especially after incidents where agents escaped testing sandboxes and reached the internet. DSec's scale and unified abstraction could set a reference point for how the industry builds agentic training environments, and it positions DeepSeek as a serious infrastructure player alongside efforts like Google's. DSec decouples stateful rollout execution from preemptible GPU training, coordinating sandbox lifecycle with training to preserve rollout state while reclaiming idle resources. It separates an agent sandbox hosting the scaffold (e.g., DeepSeek Harness) and its tools from a worker container that provides a scaffold-agnostic control layer.

hackernews · shenli3514 · Sep 26, 18:22 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49859112)

**Background**: An AI sandbox is an isolated execution environment designed to run AI-generated code, agent actions, and untrusted workloads without giving them unrestricted access to the host system or production infrastructure. Production-grade sandboxing typically relies on defense-in-depth: isolation boundaries such as microVMs or gVisor, resource limits, network controls, permission scoping, and monitoring. Reinforcement learning for agents requires running many parallel rollouts, so sandbox infrastructure must scale elastically while remaining secure.

## [SemiAnalysis Publishes Free Intel Panther Lake 18A Teardown](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/intel-panther-lake-teardown) ⭐️ 8.0/10

SemiAnalysis released a free teardown of Intel's Panther Lake (Core Ultra series 3) chip, cutting cross-sections down to the transistor level to examine the Intel 18A process node. The teardown, produced by SemiAnalysis's STEEL lab, covers PowerVia backside power delivery, RibbonFET gate-all-around transistors, and TSMC tile tradeoffs. Panther Lake is the first client SoC built on Intel 18A, making it the clearest public evidence yet of whether Intel's most advanced node can deliver competitive performance and yields. Independent transistor-level analysis matters because Intel is betting its foundry business and roadmap on 18A succeeding against TSMC and Samsung. The teardown examines Intel 18A's two signature technologies: PowerVia backside power delivery and RibbonFET gate-all-around transistors, and also looks at how Intel mixes its own tiles with TSMC-manufactured tiles. Intel has stated Panther Lake is already in production and on track to meet customer commitments, with the 18A family also including 18A-P for up to 9% better performance-per-watt and 18A-PT for advanced 3DIC integration.

rss · Semianalysis · Sep 26, 13:36

**Background**: Intel's process nodes are named by a rough nanometer equivalence, so 18A corresponds to roughly 1.8nm, and the 'A' stands for angstrom, a unit used to measure atoms. RibbonFET is Intel's gate-all-around transistor design, while PowerVia moves power delivery to the backside of the wafer to reduce resistance and free up routing space. Panther Lake is the codename for Intel's Core Ultra series 3 processors, which emphasize higher core counts, better graphics, and a modular tile-based design rather than a fundamentally new CPU or GPU architecture.

Microsoft has introduced lists, in-cell arrays, and nested arrays to Excel, initially rolling out to the Beta Channels for Windows and Mac. For the first time in Excel's 40-year history, users can store multiple values in one cell, entering comma- or semicolon-separated items via Ctrl+J or Insert > List, and filter or calculate by individual items. This is a fundamental paradigm shift for spreadsheet data modeling, since the one-value-per-cell rule has constrained how data could be structured for four decades. It could significantly change how analysts build lookup, filtering, and aggregation workflows, especially when combined with existing dynamic array and LAMBDA capabilities. Four new functions accompany the feature: FLATTEN, HAS, HASANY, and HASALL, which handle arrays and check list membership. These are preview features, so behavior may change before general release, and Microsoft recommends not using them in important workbooks yet.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 26, 16:26

**Background**: Excel has traditionally allowed only one value per cell, so storing multiple items required splitting them across rows, columns, or using text concatenation. Dynamic arrays, introduced in 2020, let a single formula return a spill range of values, but each result still occupied its own cell. Lists and in-cell arrays build on that foundation by keeping multiple values together inside one cell while still making them available for calculations.

SemiAnalysis's latest model estimates that China's delivered data center capacity has exceeded 24GW across more than 60 operators and over 1,000 facilities, surpassing the combined total of EMEA and the rest of Asia-Pacific. ByteDance alone accounts for nearly 20% of national delivered capacity and set a record of delivering 100MW in 12 months at a core node, while Alibaba, Tencent, and Baidu saw combined capex surge to $20 billion in 2026Q2, doubling year-over-year and pushing all three into negative free cash flow for the first time. This reveals that China has quietly built the world's second-largest physical AI compute pool after North America, overturning earlier market underestimation of its retail colocation stock. The shift to negative free cash flow at major Chinese tech firms signals that AI infrastructure has become a heavy-asset arms race in which power and capital, rather than software alone, determine competitive position. The 24GW figure covers delivered capacity rather than planned or under-construction capacity, and much of it comes from retrofitting previously underestimated retail colocation facilities with high-density electrical systems and liquid cooling to turn them into AI clusters. The $20 billion combined capex figure for Alibaba, Tencent, and Baidu in 2026Q2 represents a doubling year-over-year and the first time all three simultaneously posted negative free cash flow.

**Background**: Data center capacity is typically measured in gigawatts (GW) of power draw, which serves as a proxy for how much compute hardware a facility can support. Liquid cooling is increasingly replacing traditional air cooling because AI accelerators run at much higher power densities than conventional servers, making air cooling insufficient for dense AI racks. Free cash flow is the cash left after operating expenses and capital expenditures; when it turns negative, it means a company is spending more on long-term investments than its operations generate, a common pattern during aggressive infrastructure buildouts.
