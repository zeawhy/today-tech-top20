## [Chinese Scientists Confirm Existence of Glueballs for First Time](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The BESIII collaboration, led by Chinese scientists, has for the first time confirmed the existence of glueballs, a new form of matter composed solely of gluons. After 15 years of research, they verified that the particle X(2370), discovered in 2011, has properties consistent with a glueball. This discovery is a major milestone in particle physics, as it experimentally confirms a long-standing prediction of the Standard Model. It validates the theory that gluons can bind together to form new matter, which has significant implications for our understanding of fundamental forces and particles. The BESIII detector at the Beijing Electron Positron Collider (BEPCII) was used to study X(2370). In 2024, the team measured its quantum state properties, and new decay modes were found, confirming its flavor-singlet nature and that its main component is a glueball. This is considered the most definitive experimental result in the search for glueballs in nearly 50 years.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 6, 07:31

**Background**: In the Standard Model of particle physics, matter is composed of quarks and leptons, which interact via force-carrying particles called gauge bosons. Gluons are the gauge bosons that mediate the strong force, binding quarks together inside protons and neutrons. The Standard Model predicts that gluons can also bind to each other to form particles called glueballs, which contain no quarks. However, glueballs had never been observed experimentally until now.

## [Bidirectional Diffusion Models Predict Their Own Rollout Errors](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vh2gn1/roundtrip_consistency_bidirectional_diffusion/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new paper introduces round-trip consistency, a self-supervised method where a single bidirectional latent diffusion model steps a dynamical system forward and backward in time, using the round-trip discrepancy as a test-time error signal without ground truth. The method is demonstrated on CELEBV-HQ videos and turbulent plasma fields, and training both directions in one network outperforms two specialist models. This work addresses the critical problem of error accumulation in autoregressive generative models during long rollouts, where no ground truth is available at deployment. By providing a measurement-free error signal, it could improve the reliability and stability of long-horizon generation tasks in video synthesis, physics simulation, and digital twins, potentially impacting fields that rely on accurate generative forecasting. The method requires only one extra rollout (forward then backward) to compute the error proxy, avoiding ensembles, held-out data, or governing equations. The paper is available on arXiv (2608.00675), with code and project page provided, and the approach is validated on CELEBV-HQ videos and turbulent plasma fields.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Clean-Hovercraft5825 · Aug 6, 12:10

**Background**: Autoregressive generative models, such as latent diffusion or flow models, generate sequences by iteratively predicting the next step, but they accumulate errors over long rollouts. Diffusion models generate data by denoising from random noise, and latent diffusion models perform this in a compressed latent space, as used in Stable Diffusion. Round-trip consistency leverages the reversibility of the generative process to create a self-supervised error signal, similar to cycle-consistency in other domains.
