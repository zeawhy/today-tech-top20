## [Chinese Scientists Confirm Existence of Glueballs, New Form of Matter](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 6, 2026, the BESIII Collaboration announced at the International Conference on High Energy Physics in Brazil that, after 15 years of research, they have confirmed the existence of glueballs. The particle X(2370), discovered in 2011, has been identified as being dominated by glueball components, marking the first experimental confirmation of this new form of matter. This discovery resolves a nearly half-century-old puzzle in particle physics and provides crucial validation for the Standard Model, which predicted glueballs but had never observed them. It opens new avenues for studying the strong force and the fundamental structure of matter, potentially impacting our understanding of the universe. The BESIII experiment used data from 10 billion J/ψ particles to determine the quantum numbers of X(2370) as 0⁻⁺ in 2024, consistent with lattice QCD predictions for the lightest glueball. The latest results also measured its flavor-singlet nature, completing the evidence chain from mass, quantum numbers, to flavor-singlet properties.

**Background**: In particle physics, glueballs are hypothetical composite particles consisting solely of gluons, the force carriers of the strong interaction. Because gluons carry color charge, they can bind together, but glueballs are difficult to detect as they always mix with ordinary mesons. The Beijing Electron-Positron Collider (BEPC) and its BESIII detector are major scientific facilities in China used for studying particle physics in the τ-charm energy region.

## [Bidirectional Diffusion Models Predict Their Own Rollout Errors](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vh2gn1/roundtrip_consistency_bidirectional_diffusion/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The paper proposes training a single conditional latent diffusion model to step a dynamical system forward or backward in time via a direction flag, and shows that the round-trip discrepancy—the difference between the start state and the state after forward and backward rollouts—serves as a self-supervised, measurement-free error signal for autoregressive generation. This approach requires no ensembles, held-out data, or governing equations, and training both directions in one network outperforms two specialist models in both directions. This work addresses a critical problem in autoregressive generation, such as video generation and digital twins of dynamical systems, where models accumulate errors over long rollouts without ground truth to measure against. By providing a test-time error signal without extra data or ensembles, it could enable more reliable deployment of generative models in scientific and industrial applications, and the bidirectional training approach may improve model efficiency and performance. The method uses a single conditional latent diffusion model with a direction flag to enable forward and backward time stepping. The round-trip consistency error is computed by rolling forward then backward and measuring the discrepancy from the start state, providing a self-supervised proxy for rollout error. The paper demonstrates this on CELEBV-HQ video generation and turbulent plasma field digital twins, and the code and project page are publicly available.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Clean-Hovercraft5825 · Aug 6, 12:10

**Background**: Autoregressive generative models, such as latent diffusion or flow models, generate sequences by iteratively predicting the next state, but they accumulate errors over long rollouts, especially in video generation and dynamical system modeling. Diffusion models are a class of generative models that learn to denoise data, and latent diffusion models perform this in a compressed latent space, as used in Stable Diffusion. Bidirectional diffusion models, like the Bidirectional Diffusion Bridge Model (BDBM), enable transitions between two distributions using a single network, which can be more efficient than training separate forward and backward models.
